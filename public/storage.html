Research Drive storage connection

Google application: research-drive-platform · Updated 15 October 2026

An operator-managed connection for research dataset archives and encrypted recovery backups.

The research-drive-platform Google OAuth application connects the operator’s authorized Google Drive accounts to local file-transfer and backup tools used with Research Drive.

What the connection does

It supports copying selected research datasets, retrieving archived files and writing encrypted recovery snapshots. The connection is operated by the owner of this Research Drive service.

It does not create Google accounts or grant Research Drive users direct access to the operator’s Google Drive.

Google permissions

The CMS storage connection requests access to files used with this application (drive.file). Existing connections to other operator accounts can have different permissions, as shown on their Google consent screens.

Storage requests are made by local tools using credentials held on the operator’s machine.

For details about data handling, encryption, retention and revocation, read the storage privacy notice.

Contact

For questions about this connection, contact christstrife@gmail.com, the support address shown on its Google consent screen.
